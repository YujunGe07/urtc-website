§ 02 — Formats

Three ways to present.

  • Format APaper

    Research paper

    A written paper on completed or substantial research, presented as a seven-minute talk at the conference.

  • Format BCore of the day

    Poster

    Present your work in the poster sessions, the center of the conference. You talk with judges and visitors one-on-one.

  • Format CShort form

    Lightning talk

    A very short talk. Good for early results, a work in progress, or one clear finding.

Every presenter gets written feedback Our technical chairs review every presentation and send written comments. Judges score work live, attendees vote for their favorites, and awards are given at the end of the day.
