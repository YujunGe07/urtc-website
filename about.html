About

Research, out loud.

Stanford URTC, the Stanford Undergraduate Research Technology Conference, is a one-day, student-run conference where students present engineering and technology research to peers, judges and the wider research community.

§ 01 — What it is

A real conference, built for students.

Undergraduates, along with some high school researchers, present engineering and technology research through papers, posters and lightning talks.

Every presenter gets written feedback from our technical chairs.

The conference is fully in person. There is no virtual attendance or presentation option.

§ 02 — Who it's for

Who comes to URTC.

We expect about 150 to 250 attendees.

  • 01Presenters

    Undergraduate researchers

    Students doing engineering or technology research, whether in a lab, an independent project or a class.

  • 02Presenters

    High school researchers

    High school students doing engineering or technology research, including those working with a college research program or faculty mentor.

  • 03Attendees

    Anyone curious

    Students thinking about research, mentors, faculty and people from industry who want to see what students are working on.

§ 03 — Lineage

A West Coast counterpart, in the spring.

Stanford URTC is inspired by MIT URTC, which the MIT IEEE Student Branch and IEEE Boston Section have run every October since 2015.

Our relationship with MIT URTC Stanford URTC is organized independently by Stanford students. It is inspired by MIT URTC and is not an official MIT event.

§ 04 — Organizers

Run by students.

Stanford URTC is organized by Stanford students with the Stanford IEEE Student Branch, with support from IEEE Boston Section.
