Sponsors make Stanford URTC possible and get to meet the students doing research in engineering and technology early on.

§ 01 — Why sponsor

Where the research starts.

    Talk with undergraduate and high school researchers about their work, often before they've chosen a lab, a graduate program or an employer.

    Your logo on merch & banners

    Your company name and logo go on conference merchandise, banners and signage throughout the day.

  • 03 · PresenceSelect tiers

    A table at the conference

    Select tiers include a table during the conference, so your team can meet attendees in person.

    Support student research

    Your support funds written feedback, awards and an accessible event for students.
