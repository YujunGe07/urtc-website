Back the next frontier.

When you sponsor Stanford URTC, you help every part of the conference come together, and you meet the students doing engineering and technology research early on.

01 What is URTC

Research, presented by students.

Attendance
About 150 in person, plus about 50 virtualMostly undergraduate researchers
Format
A conference day, plus Sunday activitiesPapers, posters, lightning talks and a keynote on Saturday; tours, workshops and activities on Sunday
Date
May 15–16, 2027 TentativePackard Electrical Engineering Building and NVIDIA Auditorium (tentative)

Stanford URTC is the Undergraduate Research Technology Conference, a student-run event modeled on MIT URTC.

Undergraduates present engineering and technology research through paper talks, poster sessions and lightning talks, with a keynote and an awards ceremony.

The conference is run with IEEE. IEEE Boston Section is our financial sponsor, and accepted papers are published in IEEE Xplore.

02 How sponsorship works

Simple, and in kind.

  1. Pick a package

    Choose one, or combine several. Each package covers one specific cost.

  2. Talk with our chairs

    A 15-minute call with Yujun Amy Ge, Conference Chair, or Andrew Winkel, Sponsorship Chair.

  3. Cover the cost in kind

    You pay the vendor directly or ship the goods. We can't accept cash or donations.

Choose how to show up.

04 Let's talk

Talk with our chairs.

Reach out to set up a call. If you were hoping to give a check or donation, thank you. We can't accept cash, but we'd love to match you with a package instead.
